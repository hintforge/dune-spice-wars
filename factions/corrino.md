## Current status

Playable free base-game faction: Steam lists only House Vernius of Ix as paid DLC, and the official 1.0 FAQ lists six playable factions at release. It arrived via the free Conquest Update (2023-06-22 for Conquest play; the Steam news list dates the House Corrino arrival message to 2022-09-22; the exact release month was not confirmed by a page read here). Leader: Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV.

## Bonuses

Imperial Edict on Resolutions (switch scope). +1 building slot per village (6 total). Units of a faction that recently betrayed a Truce receive +10% damage from Corrino. +100% Authority to annex due to distance. At 5,000 Hegemony: Main Base Deployment once on Corrino or neutral villages; offer Imperial Mandate (gives Sardaukar to another faction). At 10,000: +5% Power per faction with fewer CHOAM Shares, and +1 Guild Favour production per such faction. v2.2.3: the 10,000 bonus no longer gives Influence for militia killed in Truce territory.
