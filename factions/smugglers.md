## Current status

Playable free faction. Playable in Conquest only since Community Update #4 (2024-11-18); the Steam beginner guide (March 2024) still said Smugglers and Fremen were excluded, which that update superseded. Leader: Esmar Tuek.

## Bonuses

Can install Underworld Headquarters in opponents' villages; +30% resources from pillaging; the Authority cost to annex does not rise with distance; +100% Authority cost from owner villages. At 5,000 Hegemony: Bounty on Resolutions, and the faction joins the Landsraad (with 50 votes; the Standing note says 200 Standing). At 10,000: sieged Smuggler villages do not resupply the occupant, and a Major Underworld HQ is installed in every enemy Main Base.
