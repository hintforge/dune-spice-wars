## Current status

Playable free faction in every mode (Conquest since 2023-06-22). Leader: Baron Vladimir Harkonnen. Feyd-Rautha Harkonnen is a councilor, not a hero.

## Bonuses

Oppression on Harkonnen villages: costs 20 Manpower; +100% resource production for 2 days; afterwards -20% production and a 3% rebel chance per day for 10 days, stacking. Can sacrifice an Agent to reduce a mission's cost and preparation time. Villages gain +5% production per active Militia; villages -10% production. At 5,000 Hegemony: 50 Intel when pillaging; a free Brainwashed Agent after killing 20 non-mechanical units. At 10,000: sacrificed Agents may not consume the operation; +1 Agent slot in each non-faction field.
