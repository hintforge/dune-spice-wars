## Current status

Playable free faction (the playable Fremen, distinct from the neutral Sietch allies every faction can recruit). Playable in Conquest since Community Update #4 (2024-11-18). Leader: Liet-Kynes.

## Bonuses

Spice fields are harvested by mobile Harvesting Teams. Can ally Sietches outside their territory. Thumpers allow Wormriding. Villages have -1 building slot. At 5,000 Hegemony: surround a Deep Desert region to control it; passive Thumpers attract larger Sandworms. At 10,000: knowledge of enemy Sietch alliances, and every allied Sietch gets a Settlement for unique buildings. Fremen never obtain Landsraad Standing (per the Standing note). Fremen cannot obtain Fuel Cells. v2.2.8.33019: Fremen Conquest main bases get a 1-slot district (Conquest only). Community Update #3 gave Fremen +1 Main Base building slot, forming a new 1-slot district.
