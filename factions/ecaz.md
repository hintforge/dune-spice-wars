## Current status

Playable free base-game faction, added with 1.0 (the official FAQ calls Ecaz "the new addition for 1.0"; 1.0 released 2023-09-14). Steam lists only House Vernius of Ix as paid DLC. Leader: Archduchess Armanda.

## Bonuses

Surrounded neutral villages become Sanctuaries (cannot be attacked by other factions; +1 Authority each; adjacent villages gain +1 trait instance). At 5,000 Hegemony: one Garden Resort, and the player can name a Champion (a non-mechanical unit that raises Hegemony by kills). At 10,000: 0.2% of Hegemony as Solari, and the first 50 votes on each Resolution are free. Ecaz start with 220 Standing.

Truce breaking: Duneopedia (undated) says Ecaz cannot betray a Truce; Ecaz could not at 1.0, and Community Update #4 (2024-11-18) says Ecaz can break a Truce with +100% Standing loss and no damage bonus. The "Reverend Mother" quest was removed for Ecaz in 2024-03 because it clashed with the no-break rule. No later change was found; the dated official note governs, but Duneopedia's contrary text is undated, so the disagreement is kept (`sections/conflicts.md`).
