## Current status

Playable free faction in every mode (Conquest since the Conquest Update, 2023-06-22). Leader: Duke Leto. Free-versus-paid check: Atreides is in the base game; Steam lists only House Vernius of Ix as paid DLC.

## Bonuses

Peaceful Annexation works on non-devastated neutral villages (standard Authority cost + 50 Influence, 5 days, global, keeps militia; not on devastated or occupied villages; Community Update #3 confirmed the devastated-village limit). Atreides villages cost +10% Authority to annex and take 10% longer to liberate. All Solari upkeeps are -10%. Cannot pillage neutral villages. At 5,000 Hegemony: +10% Solari under a positive Resolution, +10% unit Power under a negative one. At 10,000: ignore Charter prerequisites except Standing, and +2% resource production per election for a positive Resolution (x20).
