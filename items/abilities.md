# Dune: Spice Wars -- Abilities

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-07
**patch note:** numbers are Duneopedia's (undated; consistent with v2.2.3, 2024-12-04) unless a line cites an official note. Current patch v2.2.8.33026 wins; P2 re-checks.

Heroes, councilors and unique operations for the six free factions. Heroes unlock at 10,000 Hegemony; each costs 1,000 Solari (Fremen Chani and Otheym also 150 Authority), 15 days to recruit, 20 Solari upkeep. Councilors are chosen two at a time from four per faction at game start; "Hard" and "Easy" are the operation-difficulty style labels the source gives each councilor. Faction operations cost Intel (and Solari at the higher levels). House Vernius of Ix (paid DLC) has its own section at the end of the faction list below; its full file is `factions/vernius.md`.
