## Cross-faction upgrades

Barracks gear is free to install and costs Solari only to remove or swap (2024-03-07 rework; gear switching cost 1000 to 500 Solari in Community Update #3). The Armory's second gear slot is unlocked by one specific Military development per faction (Harkonnen: Adrenaline Addiction; Smugglers: Industrial Scavenging; Corrino: Imperial Command; Fremen: Stalwart Alliance; Atreides and Ecaz: Army Logistics, found in P2). The per-unit gear options for five factions are in `items/gear.md` (from the developer's changelist); House Vernius has its own section above and in `items/gear.md`.
