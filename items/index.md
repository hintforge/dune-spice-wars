# Dune: Spice Wars -- Items index

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-07

This game has no carried items. Files here hold the nearest equivalents:

- `abilities.md` -- councilors, heroes and unique operations for the six free factions and House Vernius (P3)
- `upgrades.md` -- unique developments, unique buildings, gear changes and the second Armory slot
- `developments.md` -- the 36 shared Developments (four categories, four tiers), prerequisites and the Knowledge cost formula
- `gear.md` -- Armory gear options per unit for Atreides, Harkonnen, Smugglers, Fremen, Ecaz and Vernius, with the later replacements
- `builds.md` -- generic opening advice and per-faction strategy evidence (old and thin; patch-labelled)

Design note: `abilities.md`, `upgrades.md`, `gear.md` and `builds.md` each repeat the faction list by design. They are organised by category (what a faction can pick), while `factions/<faction>.md` is organised by faction (what the faction is); each faction file points here for the full category detail, and a fact lives in one home with a pointer from the other. This is an accepted overlap, not a duplicate.

Weapons, ammo, consumables and crafting materials are absent in this game; unit rosters live in `factions/` and `mechanics.md`. Charters are in `mechanics.md`; the 38 Resolutions and Standing are in `sections/landsraad.md`.
