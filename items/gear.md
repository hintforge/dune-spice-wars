# Dune: Spice Wars -- Armory gear per unit

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-24
**patch note:** the four gear options per unit come from the developer's Heroes of Dune changelist (2024-03-07); later replacements come from the Steam patch notes through 2024-12-04. Nothing after 2024-12-04 was found to change gear, but only the Steam posts were checked, so an unlisted gear is not verified unchanged. Current patch v2.2.8.33026 wins. Corrino units, the Fedaykin, the Harpy and all later heroes and ships are not covered (gap).

## Rules

Gear is installed at the Barracks and needs an Armory slot. Slot 1 comes from Survival Training (Military tier 1); a second slot comes from a Military development (`items/developments.md`, `items/upgrades.md`). Installing gear is free once a slot exists; changing or removing a gear costs 1,000 Solari (2024-03-07), later cut to 500 Solari (Community Update #3, 2024-08-01). Rally Points can be placed anywhere within Airfield range. The condition that unlocks each individual gear is not stated in any source read (unlock: unknown). Achievements: Shiny New Gear (equip gear) and Ready to Rumble (10 gear equipped at once, which is 5 units with 2 slots each).
