## Match and lobby options

Named by sources: map size; map type; Sandworm (how fast worms wake and hunt armies and harvesters); Storm Strength (storm frequency; storms hurt units, mostly air, cut village production and block building in a hit village); Locals Strength (Sietch raid frequency and size); Spice Contract (size of the Imperial Spice Tax); Neutral Difficulty and Contract Difficulty (named only in the Insanity achievement text); number and strength of AI factions; victory conditions on or off. Lobby options also include spectators and the "Assassination tips" option (default on; Community Update #3).

The Insanity achievement wants all four of Sandworm Activity, Storm Activity, Neutral Difficulty and Contract Difficulty at their highest and every victory condition enabled. v2.2.2 added an accessibility-menu option to hide the on-screen version number.

Settings that matter for perception and difficulty: Sandworm and Storm activity (the Insanity achievement uses the highest) and the Spice Contract (tax size). Harvester Auto-Recall costs -5% Spice and tries to pull the harvester out on a worm attack; "Greed" mode, when no Ornithopter watches, gives +150% Spice during the attack but may lose the harvester.

## Accessibility (Accessibility Update, 2022-10-25)

Colorblind modes (Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia) with intensity; Magnifier (default key P, rebindable) with a zoom multiplier; Photosensitive mode (removes flashes on alerts, movement commands, explosions, Atomics and Orbital Strike effects; no sandstorm lightning; Sardaukar hit effects removed); Ally/Enemy overlay (green own, blue team, red enemy) with intensity; chat opacity and text size. Full controller support is listed on Steam.
