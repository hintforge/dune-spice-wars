# Sections -- Missables

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-07

Skirmish and multiplayer have no missable items, collectibles or story beats: maps are generated and everything is always available. What can be lost is a run, a window or an achievement condition. Each line below points to its home claim.

| What | When it is lost | Home | tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Conquest run | Pressure reaching 100% after the post-mission bonus pick loses the run even if the victory goals are met; every slow mission raises Pressure, and the latest safe point is Pressure under 50% | `sections/conquest.md` | late-game |
| Conquest capital sieges | unlocked only after quick successive wins; failing the chain or letting Pressure rise closes them | `sections/conquest.md` | late-game |
| Fremen friendship (Great-House Conquest) | looting a Sietch lowers it by 4 and plundering Ancient Sietch by 30; no source gives a loss for Fremen unity or Smuggler corruption | `sections/conquest.md` | late-game |
| Plans within Plans | the secondary objective must be completed during that mission | `achievements.md` | progression |
| Kwisatz Haderach | a Conquest win in under 4 years (whether "in-game" is meant is not confirmed); a slow run forfeits it | `achievements.md` | late-game |
| Peaceful Dune, One Against All | need a full 4-player lobby with three living opponents | `sections/multiplayer.md` | progression |
| Pariah, Savage, Prudent, Insanity | solo only; settings and conduct are fixed from the start | `achievements.md` | progression |
| No Offense | one village attack ends the attempt | `achievements.md` | progression |
| Governorship and Standing | using Nuclear Warheads costs -500 Standing and rules out the Governorship in that game | `mechanics.md` | late-game |
| Sietch alliance | an ally is lost if its Sietch is pillaged | `mechanics.md` | progression |
| Conquest territory, passive bonus, Ancient Sietch choice | a failed mission is not replayed; a lost territory, a chosen passive bonus and the protect-or-plunder choice cannot be undone | `sections/conquest.md` | progression |
| A won Governorship | a "Loss of Rights" vote revokes every active Charter except one awarded in that council | `sections/landsraad.md` | late-game |
| Vernius obfuscation | the single obfuscated development is fixed once chosen and has no effect for any faction, Vernius included; it cannot be done below 20 Standing (one Reddit source for "fixed") | `factions/vernius.md` | dlc:vernius |
