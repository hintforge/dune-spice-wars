- `conquest.md` -- the Conquest campaign: flow, win condition, Pressure, capital sieges, missions, passive bonuses
- `multiplayer.md` -- lobby, co-op, mods, multiplayer-only achievements
- `landsraad.md` -- Standing gains and losses, votes, the 38 Resolutions, the Governorship and how many days it must be held
- `progression.md` -- the patch ledger, the unlock-dependency table, cross-mode differences and common progression failures
- `conflicts.md` -- every open disagreement between sources, with all values kept, and the ones P2 and P3 resolved or corrected
- `missables.md` -- what a run or a window can lose
- `story_notes.md` -- the little story the game has (honest empty-statement)
