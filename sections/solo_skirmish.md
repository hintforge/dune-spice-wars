## Modes in this file

Battle of Arrakis is the skirmish against AI and also the standard multiplayer match ruleset ("Skirmish" in Steam patch notes): 1 to 4 players free-for-all or 2v2 teams, with empty slots filled by AI from easy to insane. Kanly Duel is 1v1 with the same rules, built to be faster: more starting resources and a small map. Five tutorials sit in the single-player menu: Basic Controls, Exploration, Siege & Expansion, Spice Harvesting, Water & Upkeep.

## Map types (skirmish and multiplayer)

Added 2024-11-18, plus Random in v2.2.2 (2024-11-26): Dune Small, Dune Medium, Dune Large (northern hemisphere of Arrakis), Great Flat (huge Deep Desert, bases far apart), Populous Basin (many small regions, bases close at the centre), Great Erg (medium, small Deep Deserts and Mountain regions), Desert Ridge (2v2, elongated), Sandy Valley (medium rectangle, 2v2 and FFA), and Random. Kanly maps: Standard Kanly and Narrow Pass (smaller, mountain-filled with bottlenecks; added 2024-11-18).

## AI difficulty

The AI difficulties are easy, medium, hard and insane; the four win achievements (Easy Win, Desert Fighter, Strategist, Master of Arrakis) map to them.

AI behaviour reported by players (early access and 1.0 era; current patch unverified): the AI avoided Sandworms by flying and used air transport (2022-04); AI factions tend to go Atreides for Governorship, Fremen for Hegemony, Smugglers and Corrino often for CHOAM; Insane AI gets stat bonuses, so fights need about 1:3 to 1:4 army ratios (2022 Chinese guide); ranged units sometimes charge into melee and units rush defences blindly (German review, 2023-09). In multiplayer the AI never offers trades outside its team and is more reluctant to accept (Community Update #3, 2024-08-01).

## Kanly Duel

Kanly is "similar to Battle of Arrakis but designed to be faster; more resources; restricted to a small map". v2.2.8.33019 fixed a Landsraad Immunity exploit that recovered all Standing in Kanly. The achievement Duelist is a Kanly win.

## Match progression gates (skirmish and standard multiplayer)

Twelve gates from the first village to the match end, for a Battle of Arrakis match. Timings are community figures with the patch they were measured on. Thresholds, Landsraad cadence and Charter rules are the first-pass facts in `mechanics.md`, repeated here only to place them in order. Unlock details: `sections/progression.md`; Landsraad detail: `sections/landsraad.md`.

| # | Gate | Entry to exit, branches and warnings | tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First village | Capture or annex your first village; Developments open only after this. The achievement Expansionist is this capture. | none |
| 2 | First harvesters and crews | Modular Parts (Economy tier 2) adds +1 max crew; Crew Training Program (Economy tier 4) adds +2 crew and +20% spice. A 2022 guide says Ornithopters attached to harvesters raise spice and detect worms [patch: 2022-05, pre-1.0; current status unverified]. | none |
| 3 | First Developments and districts | A completed 1-slot district in the main base cuts that category's Development step cost by 15% (it was 20% before the v2.0 Heroes update). Research and Trade Agreements are Development-gated. | none |
| 4 | Landsraad opens | The council convenes every 20 days with three Resolutions. A 2022 page says the window "unlocks after the first month"; the cadence was reformed later. Starting Standing: Corrino 250, Ecaz 220, Atreides 210, Harkonnen 200; the Smugglers join at 5,000 Hegemony with 200; the Fremen never. | none |
| 5 | First council vote | 1 Influence buys 1 vote, and Standing scales votes. Since v2.2.4 (2024-12-10) you cannot see others' Influence, Solari or CHOAM changes while voting, cannot buy CHOAM Shares, and sale-price drops apply at the end of the council. | none |
| 6 | First alliance or Truce | Relations are binary, Truce or Conflict. A Truce costs Authority; ending one by force costs Standing (-100 for betraying). Ecaz may break Truces since 2024-11-18, with +100% Standing loss and no damage bonus. The achievement First Step is a Truce accepted by the other side. | none |
| 7 | 2,500 and 5,000 Hegemony | 2,500 unlocks the Headquarters. 5,000 unlocks the elite unit, air units and major buildings (elites moved from 10,000 to 5,000 on 2024-03-07) and is where the Smugglers join the Landsraad. | progression |
| 8 | 10,000 Hegemony: heroes | Every faction has 2 heroes, free at 10,000 in every mode; once one is chosen the other cannot be recruited that game. A dead hero is re-bought for 1,000 Solari and 30 Standing (Fremen: 150 Authority). The achievement Favoritism is choosing a hero. [patch: 2024-03-07; later hero reworks exist; the Ghola cost is unverified on the current patch] | progression |
| 9 | District completions | 1-slot, 2-slot and 3-slot districts each add a bonus. The Statecraft (blue) district at 2 slots gives +3 Influence and +5 Intel; the great-house district at 3 slots gives +150 votes; the Fremen and Smuggler 3-slot district gives +150 max Influence and +6 Influence (v2.2.2, 2024-11-26). The achievement Full House is every base slot. | progression |
| 10 | Victory conditions | Hegemony at 30,000 (the 2023-06-22 changelist; a late-2022 guide said 25,000). Economic: more than half of CHOAM Shares. Domination: destroy the last Main Base or assassinate. Political: hold the Dune Governorship (`sections/landsraad.md`; the number of days is disputed). | late-game |
| 11 | Timings (community) | A 2022 Darkpaw guide: Insane Hegemony wins in about 8 to 9 game months and a Fremen domination in just over 9; a game month is 30 days [patch: Dec 2022, pre-1.0; current status unverified]. A 2025 competitive thread: the real combat starts around months 4 to 5, and a Fremen player reaching 20,000 Hegemony first gets dog-piled by the lobby. | late-game |
| 12 | Match end | The victory screen and stats are kept; eliminated and conceded players can spectate (up to 8 spectators in lobbies since 2024-08-01). | none |
